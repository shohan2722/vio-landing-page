TONE OF VOICE CALIBRATION
The VIO Voice: Clinical Confidence Meets Relatable Warmth


This audience is sophisticated. They detect and reject marketing language. They've been burned by over-promises. They respond to:


DO:
* Speak like an intelligent, trusted friend who happens to be medically trained
* Use specific, concrete language ("your specific biology" vs "personalized care")
* Acknowledge the emotional reality of aesthetic decisions ("we know this feels vulnerable")
* Lead with education and insight ("most people don't realize that...")
* Be direct about what we can and cannot do ("this isn't magic, it's medicine")


DON'T:
* Use hype language ("revolutionary," "game-changing," "miracle")
* Make promises that sound too good to be true ("look 10 years younger in one session")
* Talk down to the prospect or assume they don't understand their options
* Use pressure tactics or manufactured urgency
* Pretend aesthetic decisions aren't emotional and significant


The Archetype: The Trusted Medical Advisor — not the distant specialist, not the spa aesthetician, but the practitioner who combines clinical expertise with genuine care and who treats you like an intelligent partner in your own wellness journey.
